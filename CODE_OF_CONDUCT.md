# Code of Conduct

Artemis is built in the open by people who care about making a useful tool. Be someone others want to work with.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Be patient with newcomers and with questions that have been asked before.
- Give feedback that is specific and kind, and take feedback the same way.
- Assume good faith, and say so when you are not sure what someone meant.
- Keep discussions on topic: the project, its code and its users.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning language about anyone's background, identity or abilities.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Spam, advertising and repeated off-topic posts.
- Pressuring maintainers or other contributors.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space run for the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the repository (for example by opening a private security advisory and noting that it is a conduct report). Reports are handled with discretion.
